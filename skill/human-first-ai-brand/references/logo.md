# Logo

## The mark

An arched public doorway standing on the ground, with the door swung open at 30°, a handle,
and warm light in the opening. Civic architecture — a library or a market gate, not a front
door. It reads the same in every culture the movement works in.

The lit opening is enclosed by Deep Ink on all four sides. That is structural: Sunrise is
2.19:1 on cream, so it is never allowed to meet a light background directly.

All in `brand/logo/svg/`. PNGs in `brand/logo/png/`. Favicons, `.ico`, iOS and Android icons
and a web manifest in `brand/logo/favicon/` and `brand/logo/app-icon/`.

## The wordmark

"Human" ExtraBold 800 · "First" Medium 500 · "AI" Bold 700 at 0.84 cap height.
**All three in Deep Ink.** Human leads by weight; AI is smaller and quiet. That is the
argument of the brand expressed as type — never set "AI" larger, louder, or in Sunrise.

Every lockup ships as outlined vector paths, so it renders identically with no font installed.

## Clear space and minimum size

**Clear space = X**, the cap height of the "H" in "Human", on all four sides. Nothing enters
it — no text, no rule, no photo edge, no page trim, no other logo.

| | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 132px wide | 32mm |
| Stacked | 96px wide | 24mm |
| Symbol | 32px | 8mm |
| Symbol, micro build | 16px | 5mm |

Below 16px, use nothing.

## Nine misuses

1. Do not rotate or mirror it.
2. Do not stretch it — scale both axes by the same number.
3. Do not add a gradient, glow or shadow.
4. Do not recolour it outside the approved colourways.
5. Do not enclose it in a pill, badge or rounded container.
6. Do not set type inside the doorway, in any language.
7. Do not reorder the lockup. Human leads.
8. Do not use Sunrise as an outline or hairline on a light surface.
9. Do not drop it on a busy photograph — use the reversed build over a scrim, or move it.

## On photography

A scrim is mandatory and is not decoration. Deep Ink at 22% flat plus a gradient to 94% at
the bottom takes any photograph below the luminance where Warm Cream type clears 4.5:1.
Never place the mark or type over a face. If a photo will not take a scrim without going
muddy, it is the wrong photo.

## Right to left

The page mirrors — mark right, primary action left. **The symbol never mirrors.** A door
opens the same way everywhere.

## Co-branding

Match **cap height or symbol height**, not bounding box. Vertical hairline separator, 24px
padding each side at a 36px logo height. Host organisation first in reading order. Both marks
on the same surface — never one on cream and one in a white box.
