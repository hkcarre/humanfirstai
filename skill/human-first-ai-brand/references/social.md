## Four card types

**Statement** — one line, nothing else. The workhorse. If it needs a second sentence to make
sense, it is not a statement card.

**Event** — kicker, title, four facts, one action. The four facts are always *when*, *where
including access*, *what we bring*, *what you bring*. The FREE badge is not optional; cost is
the first question everyone asks.

**Stat** — one number, one sentence, one source. Never a number we cannot cite.

**Photo** — image, scrim, one line, reversed mark.

## The post itself

The image carries one idea. The caption carries the person.

1. A concrete first line — not a question, not a hook. It must survive being cut at ~140 characters.
2. A person, named, with an age and a city.
3. What actually happened, in plain words.
4. The honest limit — what the tool did not do.
5. One action, one link.
6. Three to five hashtags in CamelCase.

**Worked example:**

> You do not need to understand algorithms to benefit from them.
>
> Yesterday in Lagos, 28 grandmother traders joined a two-hour free workshop. By lunch, each
> had a digital ledger for her stock, in Yoruba and English.
>
> It did not do the counting for them, and twice it got a price wrong — which turned out to be
> the most useful part of the morning. Checking the machine is the skill.
>
> AI is not magic and it is not destiny. It is a tool made by people, and it has to serve
> people first.
>
> Free weekend workshops at your local library — humanfirst.ai/workshops
>
> #HumanFirstAI #TechForEveryone #CommunityLearning #PlainEnglishAI

## Not our voice

Hook-and-thread openers ("Most people get this wrong. 🧵"). Engagement bait ("Agree? 👇").
Unsourced metrics. Emoji rows. Emoji in headlines or near the logo. Carousels of 90pt
sentence fragments. Tagging people who have not agreed to it. AI-generated images of people.

## Accessibility, every post

- **Alt text on every image.** It is a field on every platform.
- **No text in the image that is not also in the caption.**
- **Hashtags in CamelCase** — `#HumanFirstAI`, so screen readers pronounce them.
- **Captions and transcripts on every video.** No exceptions.
- Nothing flashing, nothing that relies on colour alone.
