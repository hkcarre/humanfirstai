# 12 · Chapters and partners

## Chapter lockup

**Human First AI** · hairline rule · **City**

Use [`hfai-chapter-TEMPLATE.svg`](../brand/logo/svg/hfai-chapter-TEMPLATE.svg) — replace
`{{CHAPTER}}` with the city name and re-export. Do not rebuild it by hand.

| | |
|---|---|
| City name | Plus Jakarta Sans Medium (500), 0.84 × cap height, Deep Ink |
| Divider | 2.5u solid rule, full cap height, Deep Ink. **No opacity** — it has to survive one-colour print. |
| Padding | 0.36 × cap height each side of the rule |
| Name | The city, in the local language and spelling. München, not Munich. |

**Region, not country.** "Human First AI | Nairobi", not "Human First AI | Kenya". We work in
neighbourhoods, and a national lockup implies a mandate we do not have.

## Co-branding with partners

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| Height | Both marks optically equal. Match **cap height or symbol height**, not bounding box — a tall partner logo set to the same box height will look twice our size. |
| Separator | Vertical hairline, 1.5px, Deep Ink, full logo height |
| Padding | 24px minimum each side of the rule, at a 36px logo height. Scale proportionally. |
| Order | Host organisation first, reading order. If we are hosting, we are first. |
| Background | Both marks on the same surface. Never one on cream and one in a white box. |

If a partner's guidelines conflict with ours, theirs wins on their surfaces and ours wins on
ours. A co-branded piece follows whoever is publishing it.

## What a chapter may change

**Yes:**
- Language, idiom, examples, names, photography
- Which of the approved colourways suits local print
- Paper stock, format, whether it is a flyer or a WhatsApp card
- Adding a local partner lockup

**No:**
- The mark, its proportions, or its colourways
- The palette — including "just a slightly different orange for our city"
- The typefaces
- Adding a chapter-specific symbol, mascot or sub-mark
- Translating the name. "Human First AI" stays in Latin script even in Arabic and Japanese
  layouts, set as a foreign-script run at matched optical size.

If something you need is genuinely not possible inside these rules, that is a gap in the
system, not a reason to break it. See [13 Governance](13-governance.md).

## Funders and sponsors

- Funder logos go in a footer acknowledgement block, never in the masthead.
- Never a sponsor logo inside the clear-space zone.
- Never "Human First AI, powered by X". We are not powered by anyone.
- We do not carry commercial vendor logos on learner-facing material. A funder is acknowledged;
  a tool vendor is not advertised.
