# 01 · Foundation

## The problem we exist for

AI is the most consequential general-purpose technology since electricity, and it arrived
wrapped in jargon, hype and dread. If it only ever benefits people who already hold capital,
credentials and bandwidth, it will widen every gap it touches.

We are not here to make people enthusiastic about AI. We are here to make them **unafraid and
capable** — so the decision about whether and how to use it is genuinely theirs.

## What we actually do

Free two-hour hands-on workshops in libraries, market halls, community centres and farm
cooperatives. Run by local volunteers, in local languages, with tablets we bring. No fee,
no account, no upsell, no follow-on product.

## The four people we speak to

Everything we make serves at least one of these, and nothing we make insults another.

| Audience | What they need from us | What loses them instantly |
|---|---|---|
| **First-time and anxious learners** — older adults, informal workers, migrants, people with little formal schooling | Hospitality. Zero condescension. Visible proof that people like them are already in the room. | Jargon. Being told it is easy. Stock photos of people who look nothing like them. |
| **Volunteer educators** — the people who actually run the sessions | Material they can pick up and teach from on Saturday with no preparation | Anything that needs a designer, a licence, or a login |
| **Partners and funders** — councils, libraries, foundations, ministries | Credibility. Evidence. Something that looks accountable. | Hype. Vague impact claims. A brand that looks like a startup pitch. |
| **Policy and press** | Clear positions, honest limits, quotable plain English | Overclaiming. Techno-optimism. Doom. |

The hardest instruction in this book: **speak to a boardroom in Zurich and a community hall in
Oaxaca in the same voice.** Not two brands. One, that respects both.

## Why there is no hyphen

**Human First AI**, never *Human-First AI*.

Unhyphenated, "Human" is an independent noun making a claim: people come first. Hyphenated,
"Human-First" collapses into a compound adjective modifying "AI", which grammatically
subordinates people to the machine and lands in the same bucket as "cloud-first" and
"mobile-first". The hyphen turns a conviction into a procurement category.

It also travels better. Across Romance, South Asian and African languages the unhyphenated
phrase translates with warmth. The hyphenated one translates as technical specification.

**In every lockup:** "Human" is ExtraBold and leads. "AI" is smaller, quieter, grounded.
Never reverse the order. Never set "AI" larger than "Human". Never set either in Sunrise.

## What we are not

- Not a company. Not a product. Nothing we make has a price.
- Not an AI lab. We do not train models and we never imply that we do.
- Not neutral about harm. We say plainly when a tool is wrong, biased or unsafe.
- Not optimists by default. Useful by default.
