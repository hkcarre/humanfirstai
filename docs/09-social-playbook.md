## The four card types

**Statement.** One line, nothing else. The workhorse. Works on cream or ink. If the line needs
a second sentence to make sense, it is not a statement card.

**Event.** Kicker, title, four facts, one action. The four facts are always: *when*, *where
including access*, *what we bring*, *what you bring*. The FREE badge is not optional — cost is
the first question everyone asks.

**Stat.** One number, one sentence, one source. Never a number we cannot cite.

**Photo.** Image, scrim, one line, reversed mark. See [07 Photography](07-photography.md).

## Writing the post itself

The image carries one idea. The caption carries the person.

**Shape that works on LinkedIn:**

1. A concrete first line that is not a question and not a hook. It has to survive being cut
   off after about 140 characters.
2. A person, named, with an age and a city.
3. What actually happened, in plain words.
4. The honest limit — what the tool did not do.
5. One action. One link.
6. Three to five hashtags, lowercase except proper nouns.

**Example:**

> You do not need to understand algorithms to benefit from them.
>
> Yesterday in Lagos, 28 grandmother traders joined a two-hour free workshop. By lunch, each
> had a digital ledger for her stock, in Yoruba and English.
>
> It did not do the counting for them, and twice it got a price wrong — which turned out to be
> the most useful part of the morning. Checking the machine is the skill.
>
> AI is not magic and it is not destiny. It is a tool made by people, and it has to serve
> people first.
>
> Free weekend workshops at your local library — humanfirst.ai/workshops
>
> \#HumanFirstAI #TechForEveryone #CommunityLearning #PlainEnglishAI

## Things that are not our voice

- Hook-and-thread openers: "Most people get this wrong. 🧵"
- Engagement bait: "Agree? 👇", "Thoughts?"
- Humble-brag metrics with no source
- Emoji in headlines or in the logo lockup. One, occasionally, in a caption. Never a row.
- Carousels where every slide is a sentence fragment in 90pt
- Tagging people who have not agreed to be tagged
- AI-generated images of people. See [07 Photography](07-photography.md).

## Accessibility, every time

- **Alt text on every image.** It is a field on every platform. Fill it in.
- **No text in the image that is not also in the caption.** Alt text is not a transcript.
- **Hashtags in CamelCase** — `#HumanFirstAI`, not `#humanfirstai`. Screen readers pronounce
  CamelCase correctly.
- **Captions and transcripts on every video.** No exceptions.
- **No flashing content**, and nothing that relies on colour alone to make its point.
