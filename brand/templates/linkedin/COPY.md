### About us — 2,000 characters max

```
AI arrived wrapped in jargon, hype and dread. If it only ever benefits people who already
have capital, credentials and bandwidth, it will widen every gap it touches.

Human First AI is a volunteer movement working so that does not happen.

We run free, two-hour, hands-on workshops in libraries, market halls, community centres and
farm cooperatives. Local volunteers teach them, in local languages, on tablets we bring.
There is no fee, no account to create, no upsell and no product at the end of it.

We are not here to make anyone enthusiastic about AI. We are here to make people unafraid
and capable, so the decision about whether and how to use it is genuinely theirs.

What that looks like in a room: a trader checking her own stock figures. A farmer getting a
frost warning in time. Someone who has never had access to expert help getting some, free,
in their own language.

We are not a company and not an AI lab. We build no models and sell nothing. Everything we
make is published openly — the teaching material, the brand, the tools. Any chapter can pick
it up and run a session on Saturday without asking permission.

If you want to start a chapter, host a session, or lend us a room for an afternoon, say so
below or write to us.

AI for everyone. Opportunity for all.
```

### About — 2,600 characters max

```
I volunteer with Human First AI, a movement running free two-hour AI workshops for people
the technology was not built for.

No fee, no account, no upsell. Local volunteers teaching in local languages, in libraries,
market halls and community centres, on tablets we bring with us.

The people in those rooms are not the ones you see in AI coverage. Older adults. Informal
workers. Migrants. People with little formal schooling, and people with a great deal of it
who were simply never given a way in.

What changes is rarely dramatic and always specific. A trader checks her own stock figures.
A farmer gets a frost warning in time. Someone drafts the letter they have been putting off
for a month.

We are careful about what we claim. We do not tell people AI is easy, we do not tell them it
is safe, and we say plainly what it cannot do. The aim is not enthusiasm. It is that someone
leaves able to decide for themselves whether this is any use to them.

Everything we make is published openly: teaching material, brand, tools. A volunteer anywhere
can pick it up and run a session on Saturday, without asking anyone's permission.

If you run a library, a community centre or a cooperative and could host a session, message
me. Same if you would like to start a chapter where you live.
```

## Before you paste

- Read the four audiences in [01 Foundation](../../../docs/01-foundation.md). The same words
  have to work for a boardroom in Zurich and a community hall in Oaxaca.
- Any number you add needs a source. If it is an estimate, write "about".
- Never "AI-powered", "unlock", "seamless", "revolutionise", "leverage" or "empower". The
  full list and the reasoning are in [02 Voice](../../../docs/02-voice.md).
- No exclamation marks.
