# Alt text for the photography set

Written to the house formula: **who** + **what they are doing** + **where, and how it feels**.
Never "image of people". Never "photo of a workshop". Someone listening to this instead of
seeing it should end up with the same picture in their head.

## artisan-block-printing-tablet

- **Caption:** Learning to photograph and catalogue block-print designs.
- **Alt text:** An older woman in a deep red bandhani sari points at a tablet screen showing a block-print motif while a younger woman in blue leans in beside her, both laughing, in a sunlit textile workshop stacked with bolts of fabric.
- **Where:** Textile workshop · South Asia
- **Good for:** Small business, craft, livelihoods, intergenerational teaching.

## community-hall-session

- **Caption:** A full room, tablets shared two to a table.
- **Alt text:** Around twenty people of mixed ages sit at long tables in a bright community centre, tablets and printed worksheets between them, a facilitator in a green lanyard talking with one group while a child raises a hand nearby.
- **Where:** Community cultural centre · South-East Asia
- **Good for:** Scale, turnout, the shape of a real session. Good for event promotion.

## library-first-laptop

- **Caption:** First time writing a letter with help from a tool.
- **Alt text:** A woman with grey hair and glasses sits at a laptop in a brick-walled hall with arched windows while a young volunteer in a navy jumper leans in, pointing at the screen; both are smiling, with other learners at tables behind them.
- **Where:** Community library hall
- **Good for:** One-to-one teaching, first-time learners, volunteer recruitment.

## highland-farmer-frost-forecast

- **Caption:** Checking a frost forecast against what the field is doing.
- **Alt text:** A farmer in a knitted chullo hat holds a tablet showing a weather reading while an agricultural advisor with a notebook listens, standing between rows of potato plants on a terraced highland slope with mountains behind.
- **Where:** Terraced highland farm · Andes
- **Good for:** Agriculture, rural reach, practical daily value.

## Decorative images

If a photograph adds nothing a sighted reader would miss, give it `alt=""` so a screen
reader skips it. An empty alt on a decorative image is correct; a missing alt attribute
never is.
